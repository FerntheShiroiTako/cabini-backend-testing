Who is Cabini?

Cabini is CAB's official mascot. Created by Veronica on 2/23/25 in shitpost announcements as a meme for CAB to increase morale boost within CAB after becoming the new leader of CAB. Originally, he was created out of boredom in CAB VC on a drawing website called Sketch.io by trackpad. However, after seeing the little guy become popular over time and increase morale, she realized it wouldn't hurt to create something new out of it. That's when she announced Cabini as the official mascot, and he's been history ever since!

Where did his name come from?

Cabini's name originated from a quote, "Slava Cabini," from the previous general of the battalion, Captain Dino, on November 17, 2022, in Ehrenwache chat. This quote was used as a term for morale when addressing the chaos in CL and CLD. This later became the motto for victories and overall morale within the division.

The Lore

Feb 23rd, [REDACTED]

Field Station 29

A foggy, rainy day...


On this particular day, Veronica was walking to the officer's building to finish up working on papers for the upcoming monthly rally in her office, just as per usual, until she heard a cry nearby the thorned bushes. Curiosity got the best of her, but not with hunches that it could've been a setup to catch her off guard; she had her holster open and her hand on the gun ready in case she got attacked.

When she got closer, she noticed the crying sounded similar to a baby's wails, and when she moved the bushes to see what caused the noises. Her heart sank when she saw an abandoned baby lying in the bushes, all scratched up and barely breathing. That's when she realized that he was purposely put there to be left for dead.

Veronica dropped everything she planned for that day and urgently took him to the infirmary to get him the treatment he needed.

After a few days, Cabini's health was getting better, and Veronica had slowly grown attached to the thing. She never expected herself to be in this situation of taking care of anything, let alone a baby in a place like this. She planned to take him to the orphanage. But after picking him up and seeing Cabini's eyes widen out of joy in front of her, Veronica made her choice and chose to adopt Cabini right then and there. And let's just say her assistant, Firezilla, along with the other officers, was shocked to see the newest addition to the corps.


Thus, Cabini became part of The Battalion.
